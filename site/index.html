About

I have spent most of my working life inside small and mid-sized agencies, building software and, eventually, running the companies that build it.

In 2015 I co-founded 100Starlings, a software development cooperative, with a couple of fellow engineers — boutique, loosely joined and fully distributed, with people in four countries across Europe and South America. It is still where most of my time goes. ChaosCafe is the rest of it: the fractional CTO and advisory work I take on directly.

The name is a leftover from an early enthusiasm for chaos engineering — breaking systems on purpose to see, calmly, what they are made of. I have broadened since; the name, as far as I can tell, has not.
